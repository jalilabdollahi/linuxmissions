From the sandbox root:

  find . -name secret.conf
      prints the one path that matches: ./var/lib/billing/state/vault/secret.conf
  cat var/lib/billing/state/vault/secret.conf
      shows the file; the line you need is access_code=...
  grep '^access_code=' var/lib/billing/state/vault/secret.conf | cut -d= -f2 > answer.txt
      grep picks that line, cut keeps the text after '=', > saves it to answer.txt

(Typing the code yourself with echo LM-... > answer.txt works too.)
