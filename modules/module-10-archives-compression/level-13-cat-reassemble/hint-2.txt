  cat A B C > whole            joins files byte for byte, in the order you name them
  echo backup.part*            shows the glob order: alphabetical, so part1, part10, part11 ... part2
  ls backup.part* | sort -V    "version" sort: part2 before part10
  backup.part{1..14}           brace expansion: the shell writes out part1 part2 ... part14, in that order
  sha256sum -c FILE.sha256     recomputes the checksum and prints OK or FAILED
  tar -xzf ARCHIVE -C DIR      extract into DIR (create DIR first)
On older macOS use `shasum -a 256 -c` instead of sha256sum.
More: man cat, man sha256sum, and "Brace Expansion" in man bash
