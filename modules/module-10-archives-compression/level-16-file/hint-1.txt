Ignore the extensions completely: chunk-005.txt may be gzip and a .zip may be
something else entirely. Every format starts with its own "magic" bytes, and
there is a tool that reads them and tells you what a file really is. Try it on
a few chunks and look at the first words of its answers.
Two special cases: a gzip file can hold plain text or a whole tar archive (you
have to look inside to tell), and some chunks are empty.
