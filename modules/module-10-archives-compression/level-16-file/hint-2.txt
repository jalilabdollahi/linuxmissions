In a loop, `case "$(file -b "$f")" in gzip*) ...;; bzip2*) ...;; esac` picks a type by the first words.
The first words to expect: gzip, bzip2, XZ, Zip, POSIX tar archive, PNG, PDF, ... text, empty.
