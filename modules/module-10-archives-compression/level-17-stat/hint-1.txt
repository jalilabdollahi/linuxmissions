Each MANIFEST line is "mode mtime path", with mtime in seconds since 1970 and
the path last because it may contain spaces. `ls -l` shows neither number in
that form; you need a tool that prints a file's mode as digits and its mtime as
epoch seconds, so you can compare the strings line by line.
Be careful with lib/libacme.so.4: it is a symlink. Is the question about the
link itself or the file it points to? And what do chmod and touch do when you
hand them a symlink?
