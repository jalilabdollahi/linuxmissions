Two separate problems. Finding: the cache dirs are at different depths, some
are hidden (.cache), and some sit inside other caches (a node_modules inside
node_modules, a build inside target). Once you find an outer cache dir, you
don't want to descend into it. Measuring: the size of a directory is the total
of everything below it, in plain KiB so you can compare and sort numbers.
Is `du -sh * | sort` sorting numbers or text?
