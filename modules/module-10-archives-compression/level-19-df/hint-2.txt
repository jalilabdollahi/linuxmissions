  xz -l --robot FILE.xz         tab-separated, exact bytes; on the "totals" line field 5 is the uncompressed size

  KiB rounded up:               $(( (BYTES + 1023) / 1024 ))
df -PT columns:  Filesystem Type 1024-blocks Used Available Capacity Mounted-on   (Available = field 5, in KiB)
df -i  columns:  Filesystem Inodes IUsed IFree IUse% Mounted-on                    (IFree = field 4)
A path lives on the mount with the longest matching mount point: /srv if it is mounted, otherwise /.
In awk, $NF is the last field (the mount point). Compare numbers, not df -h output.
