  tar -tzf ARCHIVE | grep nginx           list the stored names that mention nginx
  tar -xzf ARCHIVE -C DIR MEMBER          extract only MEMBER (and everything under it) into DIR
The MEMBER must be written exactly as in the listing; if it is not, tar says
"Not found in archive". With --wildcards (GNU tar) you can use patterns, but a
loose pattern like '*nginx*' also catches logrotate.d/nginx and friends.
More: man tar
