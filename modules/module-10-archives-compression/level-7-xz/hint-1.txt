The month a log belongs to is in its name: app-2026-09-30.log is a September
log. Look at `ls -l logs | grep -- -09-30` though: the date ls shows is when
the file was last written, which for the last day of a month is already the
next month. So pick files by name, not with find -newermt or by date in ls.
Six months means six archives: a loop saves typing. Delete a month's logs only
after its archive tested fine.
