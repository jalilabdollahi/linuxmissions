The three exports line up: line 5 of each file belongs to the same host, so
the table is just the files glued side by side. Before you trust the result,
look at the bytes of each export, not just the text: a file from Windows ends
every line with \r\n, and that \r would end up in the middle of each row. For
limit.txt you need the opposite move: many lines joined into one.
