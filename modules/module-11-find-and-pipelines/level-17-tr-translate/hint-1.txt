Look at the file with its invisible characters shown (cat -A on Linux,
cat -vet on macOS): ^I is a tab, ^M is a carriage return, $ is the end of the
line. Every change here is "this character becomes that character" or "this
character goes away", which is exactly what tr does. What happens to
"Emeka D. <TAB>Nowak" if every space, dot and tab becomes _ ? What is left
to clean up after that?
