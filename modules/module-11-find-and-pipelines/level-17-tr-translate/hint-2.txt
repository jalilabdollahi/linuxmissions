tr reads standard input (tr ... < FILE) and works on single characters:
  tr -d '\r'                delete every carriage return
  tr 'A-Z' 'a-z'            map each uppercase letter to its lowercase one
                            (or '[:upper:]' '[:lower:]')
  tr ' \t.' '___'           space, tab and dot each become _
  tr -s '_'                 squeeze runs of _ into one
  tr -s ' \t.' '_'          map and squeeze in one go (SET2 is padded with its last character)
Chain several tr with pipes. More: man tr
