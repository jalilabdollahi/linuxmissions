  tr -d '\r\000\007'          delete CR, NUL (octal 000) and BEL (octal 007)
  tr -cd '[:print:]\t\n'      -c = complement: delete everything that is NOT
                              printable, tab or newline (without \n you lose the lines)
  sed 's/\x1b\[[0-9;]*m//g'   remove colour codes: ESC, [, digits and ;, then m
                              (\x1b works in GNU sed; in bash, $'\e' gives a real
                              ESC byte that every sed understands)
Remove the colour codes before the ESC byte is deleted, or match what is left.
More: man tr, man ascii (for the octal codes)
