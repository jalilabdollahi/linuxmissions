  sed -n '...p' FILE          -n: print nothing unless told to; p: print this line
  sed -n '1200,1260p'         a range of line numbers, both ends included
  sed -n '1260q'              quit at line 1260: no need to read the other 38,000
  sed -n '/RE1/,/RE2/p'       from a line matching RE1 to the next line matching RE2
  /^\[database\]$/            \[ and \] are literal brackets, ^ and $ pin the whole line
  sed '$d'                    delete the last line ($ = last line)
Combine commands in one script with ; (e.g. '5,9p;9q'). More: man sed
