Look at a few templates first (grep -rn 'old-cdn' templates | head). Count
how many URLs and dates some lines have. Then think about the pattern
itself: the URL is full of / (the usual separator of s///) and of dots, and
in a regex a dot matches any character, including the - in
old-cdn-example.com. For the dates, the parts must come out in a different
order: how can a replacement reuse pieces of what it matched?
Test on one file without -i first (sed ... templates/index.html | grep ...).
