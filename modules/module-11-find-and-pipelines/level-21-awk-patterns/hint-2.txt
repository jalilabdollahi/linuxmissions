awk runs { action } only for lines where the pattern before it is true:
  NR > 1                        skip line 1, the header
  $1 == "www-data"              the whole field equals the text (no partial matches)
  $4 > 5                        numeric comparison of %MEM
  $8 ~ /^[DZ]/                  field 8 starts with D or Z (Ds, D<, Z+ ...)
  A && B   A || B               combine conditions
The command starts at field 11 and may contain blanks, so collect fields 11
to NF:  out = $2; for (i = 11; i <= NF; i++) out = out " " $i; print out
More: man awk (on Linux: info gawk, "Patterns, Actions, and Variables")
