  find DIR -mtime -1        modified less than 1 day (24 h) ago
  find DIR -mtime 1         modified 1 to 2 days ago (find counts whole days and
                            drops the fraction), NOT "within the last day"
  find DIR -mtime +1        modified 2 or more days ago
  find DIR -mmin -1440      the same window as -mtime -1, counted in minutes
  -atime / -ctime           access time / status-change time (not what you want here)
  -type f                   only regular files
More: man find (look for -mtime and "fractional part")
