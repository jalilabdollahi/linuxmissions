Every line of etc/passwd is one account with seven fields separated by colons:
name, password placeholder, UID, primary GID, comment, home, shell.
Two of them answer the ticket. Which UID range do accounts made for people get
on Ubuntu, and which shells let someone in at all (compare with etc/shells)?
Read the UID column carefully: is root the only account with UID 0? And is
every line in the file really an account?
