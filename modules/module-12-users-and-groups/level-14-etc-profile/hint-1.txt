Read etc/profile to see how it picks up extra files and which ones. Look at how an existing
snippet in etc/profile.d does its job: what does it do besides assigning the value?
Then think about who runs it: login shells of every user, and not only bash. If the snippet
prints something or contains a bash-only construct, which logins break?
Test it the way the system would: in a clean environment, as a login shell.
