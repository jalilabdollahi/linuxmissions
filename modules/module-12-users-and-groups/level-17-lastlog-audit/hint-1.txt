Two files, one question: which regular accounts have not logged in for 90 days?
passwd tells you who counts as a regular account; lastlog.txt says when each
one last logged in. Look at the layout of lastlog.txt: are the columns separated
by single spaces, and what do the last two lines of a "never" row look like?
Also look at the time zone each login was recorded in. Is "Aug 12" the same day
for everyone?
