The columns of lastlog have fixed widths, so cut -c can take the "Latest" part
reliably where splitting on spaces breaks (empty Port or From, "Aug  4" with two
spaces). GNU date understands that format and can convert the zone for you.
  cut -c1-16 lastlog.txt                  the name column; cut -c44- takes the Latest part
  date -u -d 'Thu Aug 13 01:30:00 +0200 2026' +%F      the same moment as a UTC date
  awk -F: '$3 >= 1000 && $3 <= 59999 { print $1 }' etc/passwd
Dates in ISO form (YYYY-MM-DD) compare correctly as plain strings. Accounts that
are missing from lastlog have never logged in, too.
