A line of etc/group reads name:password:GID:member,member,... and its member
list only holds people for whom this is a SUPPLEMENTARY group.
A user's primary group is not stored in etc/group at all: it is the GID in
field 4 of their etc/passwd line. Those users are in the group too.
Also be careful what you match: is every line that contains "docker" the
docker group? And what does an empty piece after the last comma mean?
