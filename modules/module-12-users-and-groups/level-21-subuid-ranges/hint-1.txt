Each line is user:first-ID:how-many. The next free range is not "the last line plus
65536": the lines are not in order, and not every count is the same. Sort the ranges by
their start (look at the second column numerically) and find where the last one ends.
subuid and subgid are separate files with separate layouts, so work out each on its own.
And check who already has a line before adding any.
