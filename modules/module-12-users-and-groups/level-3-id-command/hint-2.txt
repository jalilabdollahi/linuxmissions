  grep -E '[=,][0-9]+\(www-data\)' id-*.txt    a group named exactly www-data, after = or ,
  grep -L PATTERN FILES                        list the files that do NOT match
  sed -e 's/^id-//' -e 's/\.txt$//'            turn id-web-01.txt into web-01
  id -u        your UID as a number
  id -g        your primary GID as a number
  id -G        all your GIDs; add -n for names
The \( \) are literal parentheses in grep -E; the closing one stops www-data-ro
from matching.
