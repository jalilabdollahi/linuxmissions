useradd does not guess: it reads the ranges in etc/login.defs, looks at the IDs
already used inside the right range, and picks the next one. Which range
applies to a person and which to a system account (-r)? Look only at the IDs
inside that range: the classic accounts below it, and the ones far above it,
do not count.
