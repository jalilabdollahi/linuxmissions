Two lists to compare: the home directories the database promises (field 6 of
etc/passwd, but only the ones under /home) and the directories that really
exist in home/. What is in one list but not the other? There are two directions,
and they mean different things (an account without a home vs. a home without an
account).
Watch the details: `ls home` hides some directories, names are case-sensitive,
an account's name need not equal its directory's name, and the sandbox has no
/home, only ./home.
