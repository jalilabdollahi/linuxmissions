Find the app's PID and look at what it has open: ls -l /proc/PID/fd. Compare that with
ls -li var/log/app. Renaming a file doesn't change its inode, and the app writes to the inode it
opened, not to the name. After the rotation something has to make the app open the new files,
and opt/app/README says what does that. The config covers two log files: how often should the
app be told?
