The token is printed once, right after the agent rotates deploy.log, and is scrubbed a few
seconds later, before trigger-deploy.sh even returns. So you have to be watching the log before
you trigger the deploy, and keep watching across the rotation. When a log is rotated, the old file
is renamed and a new one is created under the same name. Does your watcher follow the file it
opened or the name? Also: the log already contains other, revoked tokens.
