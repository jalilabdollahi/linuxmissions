less -N huge.log         line numbers on the left (also: type -N inside less)
  /deploy finished:      search forward; n = next match, N = previous match
  /panic:                search for a panic from where you are now
  &panic:                show only matching lines (& alone shows everything again)
  g / G                  top / bottom of the file;  q quits
Searching for "deploy finished:" with the colon skips "deploy finished (dry-run):".
Without less: grep -n 'deploy finished: ' huge.log gives the line numbers, and
sed -n 'FROM,TOp' huge.log prints a range of lines.
More: man less (SEARCHING), man grep
