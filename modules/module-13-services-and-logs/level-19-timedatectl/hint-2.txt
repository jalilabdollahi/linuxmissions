- timedatectl (status) shows "Time zone: Zone/Name (ABBR, +hhmm)";
  timedatectl show prints Timezone=Zone/Name for scripts.
- GNU date converts for you: TZ=<that zone> date -d '<the UTC time from kernel-oom.txt>' '+%F %T'
  prints that moment as the zone's local time (date reads the +00:00 in the input).
  Or do it by hand: date -u -d '2026-11-09 22:47:13 UTC + 3 hours 30 minutes' '+%F %T'
- grep '^YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS ' app.log finds the line of that second. The date can
  change at midnight!
More: man timedatectl, man date (DATE STRING), info date 'Date input formats'
