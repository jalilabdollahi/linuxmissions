Start with the pager text in INCIDENT.md, then look at the five sources
side by side. Which one tells you the machine's problem, and which only
the customers' problem? The unit that complains the most may be a victim:
find the very first error in the journal, in any unit, and ask what it
was complaining about. Then see which of the other sources confirms that
(kernel messages, the df snapshot) and which unit's files are the reason.
Watch the clocks: the journal export, the nginx log and your answer do not
use the same time zone.
