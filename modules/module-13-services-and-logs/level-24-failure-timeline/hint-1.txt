Do one source at a time and check each part before you merge.
For each one ask: in what zone is this time, and how precise is it? The
server's zone is what timedatectl reports; the journal's number is UTC
microseconds since 1970. syslog has neither a year nor a zone.
Convert everything to UTC once, in the same text format: then plain string
comparison works for the window and for sorting.
Which syslog lines are copies of journal lines? Compare a few by eye first.
And what does `sort` do with two lines that have the same time?
