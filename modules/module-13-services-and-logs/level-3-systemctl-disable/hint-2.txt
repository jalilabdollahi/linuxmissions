systemctl is a mock with the real verbs:
  systemctl stop UNIT                        stop it now
  systemctl disable UNIT                     remove the [Install] links (add --now to also stop it)
  systemctl list-dependencies --reverse UNIT which units pull this one in (Wants=, Requires=)
  systemctl mask UNIT                        link /etc/systemd/system/UNIT to /dev/null: nothing,
                                             not even a Wants= of another unit, can start it
Check the result with systemctl status UNIT (Loaded: masked) and ls -l etc/systemd/system/.
Read more: man systemctl (disable, mask), man systemd.unit (Wants=)
