Find who pulls it in, then stop, disable and mask it:

systemctl list-dependencies --reverse legacy-sync
systemctl disable --now legacy-sync
systemctl mask legacy-sync
ls -l etc/systemd/system/ etc/systemd/system/multi-user.target.wants/
systemctl status legacy-sync

- list-dependencies --reverse   shows report.service: its Wants= would start legacy-sync again
- disable --now                 removes the multi-user.target.wants link and stops the process
- mask                          creates etc/systemd/system/legacy-sync.service -> /dev/null, which
                                hides the packaged unit in usr/lib/systemd/system completely
