journalctl (a mock, real options) filters on the entry's fields:
  -p err                 priority err (3) and everything more serious: crit (2), alert (1), emerg (0)
  -p warning..err        a range, if you ever need one
  --since "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM"   a full date and time (local server time); "HH:MM" alone means today
  --list-boots, -b       boots in the journal; -b limits the output to the current boot (not wanted here)
  -o short-iso           timestamps like 2026-11-10T01:02:03+01:00
Read more: man journalctl (-p, -S, -b, -o), man systemd.time (time formats)
