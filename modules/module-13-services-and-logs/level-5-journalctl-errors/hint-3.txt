Filter by priority and a full start date, print ISO timestamps, save the result:

journalctl -p err --since "2026-11-09 23:40" -o short-iso > errors.txt
cat errors.txt

- -p err                         err, crit, alert, emerg; PostgreSQL "ERROR:" lines are logged at
                                 warning and pgbackrest's stdout at info, so they stay out
- --since "2026-11-09 23:40"     yesterday 23:40; "--since 23:40" would mean today at 23:40
- (no -b)                        both boots: before and after the reboot at around 00:30
- -o short-iso                   date, time and zone on every line
