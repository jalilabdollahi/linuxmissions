journalctl (a mock, real options):
  -u payment.service           every entry of that unit: its processes, whatever they call
                               themselves, plus systemd's own messages about it
  --since "2026-11-10 HH:MM"   start of the window
  --until "2026-11-10 HH:MM"   end of the window
  -o cat                       the message only, without time, host and identifier
Compare with -t payment-api (one identifier only) or grep payment to see the difference.
Read more: man journalctl (-u, -S, -U, -o)
