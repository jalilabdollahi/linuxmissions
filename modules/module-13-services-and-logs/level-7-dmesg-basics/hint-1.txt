Look at captures/dmesg.txt: each crash leaves a "segfault at" line naming the program (with its
PID) and, after " in ", the file the faulting instruction was in. A few report-renderer lines are
not crashes.
The number in [brackets] is not a time of day: it's seconds since the kernel booted. Which file
tells you when the machine booted? Watch the brackets too: small numbers are padded with spaces.
