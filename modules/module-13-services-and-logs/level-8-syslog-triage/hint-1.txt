Every syslog line is: month, day, time, host, then the program tag ("prog[pid]:" or just "prog:"),
then the message. Three things will mislead a quick count:
- only one hour counts, and the file covers more than that;
- rsyslog doesn't write identical messages again and again: look for lines that say how many
  times the previous message was repeated;
- is "orders-api[2231]:" a different program from "orders-api:"?
