Only two kinds of sshd lines decide this: failed password attempts and accepted logins. The
order matters: the failures have to come *before* the login from the same IP. Read the log from
top to bottom, keep a running count of failures per IP, and look at that count each time a login
succeeds.
Look closely at a few failure lines: they don't all have the same number of words, and some of
them stand for more than one attempt.
