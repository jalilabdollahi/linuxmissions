rsync is the tool. Its "archive" option -a is a bundle: -rlptgoD (recursive, symlinks as symlinks, permissions, times, group, owner, devices), but not everything.
  -H           preserve hard links
  -n           dry run: change nothing
  -i           itemized output, one line per difference
The source's trailing slash matters: `rsync -a www new-server/www` and `rsync -a www/ new-server/www/` do different things (the first puts www inside the target, the second its contents).
For the second run, combine -n and -i and redirect the output to second-run.txt.
More: man rsync
