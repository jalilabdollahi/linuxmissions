Before extracting anything, ask what a plain extract does when a file of the same name is already there. Is that what you want for the files people edited today?
You want only the files that are gone. Can the extraction tool skip files that already exist, or can you restore into a scratch place and copy over only what is missing?
After the restore, compare modes and modification times with what the archive says (`tar -tvzf` shows them). A normal user's umask can change permissions during extraction unless you ask otherwise.
