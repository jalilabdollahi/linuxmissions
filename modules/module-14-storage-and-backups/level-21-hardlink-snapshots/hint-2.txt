  rsync -a SRC/ DEST/                   copy a tree keeping modes, times and links (trailing slash on SRC: copy the contents)
  rsync -a --link-dest=DIR SRC/ DEST/   files identical to the ones under DIR become hard links to them
  ls -li / stat -c %i FILE              the inode number: equal numbers mean one file
Careful: a relative --link-dest path is resolved from the destination directory, not from where you stand. If rsync cannot find it, it just makes a full copy and does not complain.
