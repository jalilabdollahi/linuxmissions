Each schedule becomes five time fields (minute, hour, day of month, month, day of
week) followed by the command. crontab-fields.txt has the order.
Take each schedule word by word: which field does "every 15 minutes" restrict, and
which ones do "Monday to Friday" and "January, April, July and October"? A field
you do not need to restrict is *.
