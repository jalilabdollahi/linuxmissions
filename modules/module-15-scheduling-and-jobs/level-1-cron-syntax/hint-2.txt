Field syntax (man 5 crontab):
  *        every value              1-5     a range (inclusive at both ends)
  */15     every 15th value         1,4,7   a list
  9-16     hours 9 to 16            mon-fri, sat,sun, jan,apr   names work too
A range is inclusive: the hour range 9-17 includes 17:00, 17:15, 17:30 and 17:45.
"On the hour" needs minute 0; a * in the minute field means every minute of that hour.

Check your file with the sandbox helper:
  cron-expand --from "2025-01-06 08:00" --days 1 schedule.cron
prints every time each line would fire that day (line number in the second column).
