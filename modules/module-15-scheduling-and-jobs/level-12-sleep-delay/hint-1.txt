The script is a loop: ask ./probe, stop as soon as it succeeds, otherwise pause and ask
again. Two more things decide whether it is a good loop: how long it pauses between
tries, and how it knows that the time is up. bash keeps a counter of the seconds since
it was reset, so you don't need to do arithmetic with dates.
