You can't type ./switch-traffic.sh at the right moment by hand: you need a
command that keeps checking spool/ and returns as soon as it is empty, so
the switch can run right after it on the same line.

Before you start the uploader, look at spool/ the way the uploader sees it.
Does plain `ls` show every entry? What does "empty" mean for a directory
that also holds in-flight uploads?
