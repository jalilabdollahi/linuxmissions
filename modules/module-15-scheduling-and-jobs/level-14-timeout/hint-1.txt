Try a few hosts by hand: ./healthcheck.sh web-01.prod; echo "exit $?"
Some answer at once, some fail at once, some never return (Ctrl-C stops
them). You need a way to start a command with a deadline, and a way to tell
afterwards whether it finished on its own or was stopped. Its exit status
is the place to look.

Afterwards, check that nothing was left behind: ps -ef | grep healthcheck
