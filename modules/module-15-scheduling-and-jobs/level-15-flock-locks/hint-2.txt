flock(1) takes an advisory lock on a file; the lock belongs to the open file descriptor
and disappears when every process holding it has exited.
  exec 9> sync.lock      opens fd 9 on the lock file for the rest of the script
  flock -n 9             -n = do not wait: fail at once (exit 1) if someone else holds it
Put this near the top of sync.sh, right after the `cd`, and decide what to print and which
status to exit with when flock fails. man flock has the details.
To try it: SYNC_SECONDS=5 ./sync.sh & ./sync.sh; echo $?
