Guard: `set -u` makes an unset variable an error; `: "${CACHE_DIR:?message}"` stops the
script with a message when the variable is unset OR empty; `[ -d "$CACHE_DIR" ]` checks it
is a directory. Quote "$CACHE_DIR" everywhere.
Selection (see man find): -mindepth 1 skips the starting directory itself, -type f only
matches regular files (not directories, not symlinks), -mtime +7 means "older than 7 days",
! -name .keep spares the .keep files, and -delete removes without a pipe through xargs, so
names with newlines or spaces are fine. find does not follow symlinks unless asked (-L).
