Work through the timers one by one and ask the same four questions of each: is every section
header spelled the way systemd knows it? Does OnCalendar describe a time that exists (or
does the timer use another trigger)? Which service does it start (the timer's own name,
unless a Unit= says otherwise), and does that file exist? Is there an [Install] section that
lets `systemctl enable` hook it into timers.target? A loop over the files with grep does the first
and last checks for all eleven at once, and ls shows the services. Not every unusual-looking
timer is broken.
