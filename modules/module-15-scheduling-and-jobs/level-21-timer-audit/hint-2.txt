Ways to check all of them at once:
  grep -L '^\[Timer\]' etc/systemd/system/*.timer          files without a proper [Timer] header
  grep -L 'WantedBy=timers.target' etc/systemd/system/*.timer
  grep -H OnCalendar etc/systemd/system/*.timer             then test each spec:
  systemd-analyze calendar '*-*-* 03,15:00:00'              prints the next elapse or an error
For the service: foo.timer starts foo.service unless it has Unit=...; compare ls *.service with the timers.
A timer with OnBootSec=/OnUnitActiveSec= and no OnCalendar is a valid monotonic timer.
Fix a name with `mv` or a `Unit=` line; fix a header with sed -i.bak; append [Install] with printf >>.
