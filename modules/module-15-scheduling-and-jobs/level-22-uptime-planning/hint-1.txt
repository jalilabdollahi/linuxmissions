Think in time spans, not in single lines. The box is down from each `shutdown` record until
the next `reboot` record. Which of the 14 times 02:00 fall inside such a span, and which
nights have no shutdown record at all? Look at how the crash night looks in the file and at
what the session line before the next reboot says; the ticket tells you when it went down.
