Plan before you edit. First find out which jobs are heavy: that needs a number per job
from durations.log, and PLAN.txt says exactly which one (a percentile, not the average or the
maximum, and exact job names). Then look at crontab.txt as a whole: which lines fire on
Monday inside the window (a `1-5` or `*` in the weekday field counts), and are there lines you
might have missed, one of which doesn't even look like it belongs to the night? Finally lay the
heavy runs end to end on paper, in an order that respects the export-before-report rule.
