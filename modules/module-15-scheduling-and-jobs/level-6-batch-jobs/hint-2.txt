batch                queue the commands on stdin in queue b (runs when the load is low)
echo 'CMD' | batch   the same without the at> prompt (this mock cannot show it)
at -c N              the job's script: saved variables, cd DIR, then your commands
atq, atrm N          list jobs / remove a job that would fail
at and batch save the EXPORTED variables and the current directory at the moment you
queue the job. A plain VAR=value without export is not saved.
More: man at (batch is described there), help export
