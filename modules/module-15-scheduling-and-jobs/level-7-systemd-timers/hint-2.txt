purge.service:  [Service] with Type=oneshot and ExecStart=/opt/app/purge.sh
purge.timer:
  [Timer]
  OnCalendar=WEEKDAYS YEAR-MONTH-DAY HH:MM:SS   weekdays as Mon..Fri or Mon,Tue,...
  Persistent=true      if the box was off at the scheduled time, run at next boot
  [Install]
  WantedBy=timers.target
Check a spec before using it (this mock only knows the calendar verb):
  systemd-analyze calendar --iterations=5 'Sat 10:00'
"daily" means *-*-* 00:00:00, every day.   More: man systemd.timer, man systemd.time
