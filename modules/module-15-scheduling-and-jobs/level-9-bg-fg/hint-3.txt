./export.sh &
kill -STOP %1
./snapshot.sh
bg %1
wait %1

- ./export.sh &        job [1] starts writing heartbeats to export.log
- kill -STOP %1        pauses job 1: no more heartbeats, jobs shows it as Stopped
- ./snapshot.sh        runs for about 5 s while the export is paused
- bg %1                SIGCONT: the export continues in the background, counter intact
- wait %1              blocks until the export has written DONE (fg %1 works too)
