The comment at the end of a key line is just free text anyone can type: it proves nothing.
What identifies a key is its fingerprint, and offboard.txt gives you three of them. How can
you print the fingerprint of every key in authorized_keys? Compare, then remove the matching
lines only; lines for people who are staying must stay character for character.
For the CI key, think of everything it should be forbidden from doing, and which options
in front of a key line enforce that (see the sshd man page, section AUTHORIZED_KEYS FILE FORMAT).
