Three things make this service dangerous: who it runs as, what that account can do on the whole system,
and what it can change among its own files. Fix them one at a time: look at the passwd line of the account the
unit uses, at the modes under opt/reportd (who may write?) and at what the service really needs to write.
Run bin/reportd-check after each step; it tells you what the service could not do.
