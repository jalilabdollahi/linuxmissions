You need to ask find for directories that are writable by "others" and do not have
the sticky bit (the `t` at the end of `ls -ld`). Remember that files and symbolic
links also appear in a search and can look world-writable, so restrict the search to
real directories. One directory has a space in its name: be careful when you loop over
the results. Save the list before you change anything, because afterwards the same
search finds nothing.
