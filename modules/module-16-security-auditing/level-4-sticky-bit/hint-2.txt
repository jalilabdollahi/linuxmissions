find can test permission bits: `-perm -0002` means "all these bits are set" (here: others
can write), and `! -perm -1000` means "the sticky bit is not set". Combine them with
`-type d`. Then read the list line by line, not word by word:
  find srv -type d -perm -0002 ! -perm -1000 | sort > fixed.txt
  while IFS= read -r d; do chmod +t "$d"; done < fixed.txt
Check with `ls -ld` on a few directories: the last permission character becomes t.
More: man find (the -perm section), man chmod
