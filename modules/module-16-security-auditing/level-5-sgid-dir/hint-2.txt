`chmod g+s` sets the setgid bit. To apply it to directories only, let find choose them:
  find projects -type d -exec chmod g+s {} +
`{} +` hands many names to one chmod call and copes with spaces in names. Hidden
directories like projects/.archive are found too, unlike a shell glob such as projects/*/.
Check with `find projects -type d ! -perm -2000` (should print nothing) and
`find projects -type f -perm -2000` (also nothing).
More: man chmod, man find
