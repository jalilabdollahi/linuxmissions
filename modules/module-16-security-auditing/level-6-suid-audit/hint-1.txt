Two lists have to be compared: what the image really contains and what the vendor
says it should contain. The first one comes from searching the file tree for the
special permission bits (look for `s` in the `ls -l` output of the usual suspects).
Search the whole of rootfs/, including hidden folders, and do not trust names: a
program called pkexec in the baseline may live at another path in the image.
Remember the baseline starts with comment lines and its paths begin with `/`.
