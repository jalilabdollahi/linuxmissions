- The first summary line ends with three load averages: 1, 5 and 15 minutes.
- %Cpu(s) lists us, sy, ni, id, wa ...; wa is time waiting for I/O.
- The Tasks line counts running, sleeping, stopped and zombie tasks.
- RES is in KiB when it is plain digits, MiB with an "m" and GiB with a "g".
  Convert everything to one unit (awk can strip the suffix and multiply)
  before comparing; the PID is column 1.
- grep -o 'pattern.*' prints from the match to the end of the line, and
  awk '{print $N}' picks column N. man awk, man top (the "Memory Usage" section).
