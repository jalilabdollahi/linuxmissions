- ss -ltn lists listening TCP sockets in numeric form. On a LISTEN socket
  Recv-Q is the number of connections currently waiting in the accept queue
  and Send-Q is the backlog, the queue's maximum size. man ss, section on
  Recv-Q/Send-Q.
- A queue is full when it holds more than the backlog allows. A busy queue
  and a small backlog are both normal on their own.
- ss -ltnp shows which process owns each listener (only your own).
