- df -h reports blocks (bytes), df -i reports inodes (one per file). Look at
  the IUse% column of captured/df-i.txt.
- find spool -type f -printf '%h\n' prints, for every file, the directory it
  is in (%h). Count identical lines with sort | uniq -c, then sort -rn to put
  the biggest on top. Piping through xargs would break on names with spaces.
- du -sh would rank directories by bytes, which is a different question.
