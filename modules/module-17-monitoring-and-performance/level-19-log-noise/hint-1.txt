You cannot see templates in raw lines because every line differs in its
numbers. First make the lines comparable: strip the timestamp and replace the
parts that change (numbers, IDs, addresses) by a fixed marker. Then count how
often each normalised line occurs and look at the top of the ranking, and at
the lines just below it.
Then write patterns that are as specific as the template: the log level and
the fixed words, not just a keyword.
