- ps -eo pid,rss,vsz,cmd prints exactly the columns you ask for; RSS is the
  resident set (KiB), VSZ the virtual size.
- --sort=-rss sorts by a column, and the minus sign means biggest first.
- Keep only the workers with grep on their command line (the script is
  opt/workers/worker.py), and take the first three with head -3.
- awk '{print $1}' keeps only the first column (the PID).
- man ps (section STANDARD FORMAT SPECIFIERS and the --sort option).
