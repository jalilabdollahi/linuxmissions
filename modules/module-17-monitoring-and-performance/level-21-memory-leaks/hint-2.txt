- The resident memory of a process is the VmRSS line in /proc/PID/status
  (in KiB), or `ps -o rss= -p PID`. pgrep -f opt/worker.py lists the four PIDs.
- Record PID and RSS for all workers into a file, sleep for 10 seconds or more,
  record again, and subtract: (second - first) / seconds = KiB per second.
- join file1 file2 pairs the lines by PID; awk does the arithmetic.
- Tiny differences (a few KiB) are noise: only steady growth is a leak.
