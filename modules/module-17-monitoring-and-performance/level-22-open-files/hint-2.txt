- ls /proc/PID/fd | wc -l counts a process's open descriptors.
- /proc/PID/limits has the line "Max open files": the first number is the
  soft limit (what is enforced), the second the hard limit (the ceiling the
  process may raise the soft one to). `ulimit -Sn` / `-Hn` show the same for a shell.
- readlink /proc/PID/fd/N shows the target: "socket:[inode]", "pipe:[inode]"
  or a path for regular files. Count the kinds for the process in danger.
- pgrep -f opt/svc.py lists the four PIDs. awk can divide count by limit.
