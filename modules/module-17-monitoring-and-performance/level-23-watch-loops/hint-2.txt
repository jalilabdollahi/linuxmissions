- /proc/PID/stat: utime is field 14 and stime field 15 (man proc), counted
  from the PID. The name in field 2 is in parentheses and may contain spaces
  and even parentheses itself, so split after the LAST ") ": with
  sed 's/.*) //' the remaining fields start at the state (field 3), and utime
  and stime become the 12th and 13th words.
- RSS: awk '/^VmRSS/ {print $2}' /proc/PID/status (KiB).
- date +%s is the epoch; sleep 1 between samples (not after the last one).
- If a read fails because the process is gone, `|| exit 0` ends the script
  quietly; send errors of the reads to /dev/null.
