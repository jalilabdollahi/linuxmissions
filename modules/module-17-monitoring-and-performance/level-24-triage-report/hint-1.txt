Treat it as four separate investigations and keep notes as you go.
CPU: a snapshot never shows short-lived children; sample and follow the parent.
Memory: size is not growth; measure twice with a pause in between.
Network: which listener's queue of waiting connections is full?
Disk/log: which file is growing, who has it open, and who restarts that process?
Order matters at the end: some facts disappear when a process does, and a
process with a supervisor is not stopped by killing just the child.
