- In `free`, "free" is memory nobody uses at all; Linux fills the rest with
  buff/cache (disk cache it hands back when a program asks). "available" is
  the estimate of what programs can really get. meminfo2.txt calls it
  MemAvailable (in kB, free -m shows MiB).
- Swap used is the "used" column of the Swap: line. Compare it in the two files.
- 10 % of total: multiply available by 10 and compare with total (shell
  arithmetic $(( ... )) or awk both work).
- awk '/^Swap:/ {print $3}' file prints one column of one line. man free.
