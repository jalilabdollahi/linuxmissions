- The swap-rate columns are si (swapped in from disk) and so (swapped out to
  disk), both per second. wa is the percentage of CPU time waiting for I/O.
- A sample line starts with a number, a heading line starts with a letter
  (procs / r). In awk, $1 ~ /^[0-9]/ selects only the samples.
- Count samples with a variable (n++) in awk, and count matches with a condition
  such as $16 > 30 (count the columns of the header to find wa).
- `awk '$1 ~ /^[0-9]/ && COND' file | wc -l` counts matching samples.
  man vmstat has a description of every field.
