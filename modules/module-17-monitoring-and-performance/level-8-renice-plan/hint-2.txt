- renice -n 15 -p PID [PID ...] sets the niceness of those processes to 15.
- renice -n 15 -g PGID does the same for every member of a process group;
  ps -eo pid,pgid,ni,cmd shows the PGID column.
- renice -n 15 -u USER hits every process of a user: too broad here, and it
  errors on processes whose niceness would have to go down.
- Select the targets the way you did in the audit: batch scripts with
  NI below 10 (ps + awk). A for loop over the PIDs keeps it short.
- man renice.
