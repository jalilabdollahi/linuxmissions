Look at what etc/resolv.conf really is (`ls -l etc/resolv.conf`) and what its
first lines say about who writes it. A file that is generated is overwritten when
the generator runs. Then find where systemd-resolved reads its settings from, and
which of those files belongs to the package and which are meant for local changes.
