systemd-resolved reads etc/systemd/resolved.conf and then every *.conf in
etc/systemd/resolved.conf.d/, in alphabetical order. Put your settings in a new
drop-in there, under a [Resolve] section, with `DNS=` and `Domains=`.
Careful: DNS= and Domains= are lists, and a later file adds to the list instead
of replacing it. An empty assignment (`DNS=`) clears the list so far. Look at the
other drop-in before you write yours. Apply with `systemctl restart systemd-resolved`
and verify with `resolvectl status`. More: man resolved.conf
