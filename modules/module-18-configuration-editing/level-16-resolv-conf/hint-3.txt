cat > etc/systemd/resolved.conf.d/20-corp.conf <<'CONF'
[Resolve]
DNS=
DNS=10.20.0.53 10.20.0.54
Domains=
Domains=corp.example lab.example
CONF
systemctl restart systemd-resolved
resolvectl status

- [Resolve]                 the section systemd-resolved reads; keys outside it are ignored
- DNS= / Domains= (empty)   clear what the vendor drop-in added, so the old server and domain do not stay
- the two real assignments  the new servers and the search order, as the ticket asks
- systemctl restart ...     makes systemd-resolved regenerate the resolv.conf files
- resolvectl status         shows what the resolver uses now
