In exports(5) a line is `path client1(options) client2(options)`: the options
belong to the client they touch. A space before the "(" ends that client, and the
parenthesised part then applies to a new client without a name, which means "any
host". Remove that space. For the ISO mirror, take no_root_squash out of the option
list (root_squash is the default). exportfs reads the file only when told to: `exportfs -r`
re-exports everything, `exportfs -v` shows the result. More: man exports, man exportfs
