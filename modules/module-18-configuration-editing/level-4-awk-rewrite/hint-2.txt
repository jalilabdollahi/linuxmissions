awk splits each line into fields ($1..$NF). When you assign to a field, awk
rebuilds the whole line with single spaces, which destroys alignment. Print the
line yourself with printf and the column widths instead:
  printf "%-12s %-15s %-5s %-7s %s\n", $1, $2, $3, 0, $5
(%-12s = left-aligned in 12 characters). Use a pattern to choose the rows, and
`next` or a final `{ print }` rule to let all other lines through.
awk can't edit in place: write to a new file, then `mv` it over the old one.
More: man awk
