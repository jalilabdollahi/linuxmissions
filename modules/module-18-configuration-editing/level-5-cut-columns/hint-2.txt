cut -d= -f1 prints field 1 when the line is split at every "=". To get everything
after the first "=", ask for a range of fields: -f2- means "field 2 to the end",
which also keeps the later "=" signs of a base64 value.
  grep -v '^#' file        drop comment lines (-e can be repeated for blank lines: '^$')
  sed 's/^export //'       remove a prefix
  grep '^NAME=' file       select one setting
Chain them with pipes and redirect the result with >. More: man cut
