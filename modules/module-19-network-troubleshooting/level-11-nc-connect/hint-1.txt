Test one port by hand first and notice the three possible outcomes: the connection is accepted, it is refused at once, or nothing happens at all. A probe without a time limit can wait for minutes on that last kind, so set one.

The status backend accepts the connection and then says nothing: it waits for you to speak first. Connecting and waiting for a greeting gets you nowhere (press Ctrl-C if you get stuck). Send it the command, then read the answer.
