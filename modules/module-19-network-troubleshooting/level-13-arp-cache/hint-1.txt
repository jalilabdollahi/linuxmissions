Put the three captures side by side and ask, for each VLAN 30 address (dev eth0.30): how many DIFFERENT MAC addresses did it have over the 10 minutes?

Before you trust a difference, look closely at the 09:05 capture and at the lines without a MAC. Is "AA:BB" really a different address from "aa:bb"? What does a FAILED entry tell you about the MAC?

Once you have the address, the inventory tells you which of its two MACs is ours, and the first three bytes of the other one name its maker.
