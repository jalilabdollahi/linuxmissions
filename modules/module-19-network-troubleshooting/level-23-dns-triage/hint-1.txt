"One in three" and "a retry works" are clues on their own: what in etc/resolv.conf makes consecutive lookups behave differently? How many of something are there?

Asking "the DNS" is not enough here; ask each server separately, by the names the failing requests use, not www. And there is one record every zone has that tells you, in one number, how up to date a server's copy of the zone is.
