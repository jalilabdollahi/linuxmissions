  diff OLD NEW         show what must change to turn OLD into NEW
  -u                   unified format: --- old / +++ new header, @@ hunks,
                       '-' removed lines, '+' added lines, 3 lines of context
  > changes.diff       save the output
To test a patch: cp v1.conf test.conf && patch test.conf < changes.diff && diff test.conf v2.conf
