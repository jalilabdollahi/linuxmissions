apt-cache policy agentd
apt update
apt-cache policy agentd | awk '/Candidate:/ {print $2}' > candidate.txt

- policy (before)   Candidate is still the old version: the local index is stale
- apt update        re-reads the vendor repository's index; it does not install or upgrade anything
- policy | awk      prints only the Candidate version; > candidate.txt saves it
