apt-get -s upgrade        -s simulates: prints the plan, changes nothing
apt-get -s full-upgrade   the same plan, but allowed to install new dependencies and remove packages

Compare the two: whatever upgrade keeps back and full-upgrade then does is what
needs a human look. Pick the names out of the "The following packages have been
kept back:" paragraph (indented lines under it) into the file, one per line.
More: man apt-get
