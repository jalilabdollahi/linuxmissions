Run it and read the errors: bash quota_report.sh usage.tsv

Then ask, line by line:
- What does the first line of usage.tsv contain, and what does bash do with a
  word like `used_kib` inside $(( ))?
- Try   echo $(( 08 ))   and   echo $(( 0755 ))   at the prompt. What does a
  leading zero mean to bash? Which of the two is worse: the error, or the
  number that is silently different?
- With whole numbers only, what is 943 / 1048 ? And then times 100?
- Is "divide every user by 1024, then add" the same as "add, then divide"?

Game tip: `edit quota_report.sh` opens the script in an editor.
