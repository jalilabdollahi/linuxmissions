Read README.vendor: is 03/11/2026 the 3rd of November or the 11th of March?
Tools like date -d guess American month/day and don't complain when the day
is 12 or less. There are three styles; each needs its own rule, and you can
tell them apart by a character: "/", " " or "-". Change only the checkin
column; the guest column has quoted commas, so check you rebuild the rest
of each line unchanged.
