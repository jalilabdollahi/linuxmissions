This is set arithmetic. Make three clean lists, one name per line: who has a seat,
whose account is enabled, who logged in successfully. Then "active" is the
intersection of the last two, and the answers are differences between "licensed"
and "active".
Before comparing, make the names comparable: look at them with cat -A. What about
upper case, spaces at the end, the comment line, users who appear 9 times in the
log, failed logins, and lines where user= is not in the same column?
