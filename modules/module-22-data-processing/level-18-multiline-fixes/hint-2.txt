Quote parity: outside a field's quotes you have seen an even number of " in total.
An escaped quote "" adds two, so it never changes even/odd.
In awk:
  n = gsub(/"/, "&")            counts the quotes on the line ("&" puts each one back)
  q += n; q % 2                 odd = still inside a quoted field, the record goes on
  rec = rec " " $0              glue the next line on with one space
When q is even again: print rec, reset rec and q.
Check: tail -n +2 tickets_flat.csv | wc -l, and look at a record that had notes.
gawk's FPAT parses the fields of a joined line if you need them later.
More: info gawk "Splitting By Content"
