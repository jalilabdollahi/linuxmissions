Make the invisible visible: cat -A users.tsv | head shows tabs as ^I, line ends as $
and carriage returns as ^M. Some "spaces" show up as M-BM- : those two bytes (c2 a0
in od -c / xxd) are a non-breaking space, a different character from the normal
space (20). Trimming that only knows about ' ' or [[:space:]] leaves them in place.
So: first turn the odd characters into normal ones, then trim each field, then drop
the repeated users. Work per field, not per line: a tab-separated line has a
padded field in the middle too.
