  sort -t, -k3,3        sort on field 3 only, fields separated by commas
       -n               numeric: 12 > 9, but it stops at the "e" in 1.2e3
       -g               general numeric: also reads 1.2e3, 4.75E2, -2.5e1
       -r               reverse: largest first
