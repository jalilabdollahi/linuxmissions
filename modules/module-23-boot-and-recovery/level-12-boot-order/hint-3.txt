./efibootmgr -v
grep -i PARTUUID blkid.txt
./efibootmgr -o 0003,0000,0004,0001,0002

- -v shows Boot0003 (ubuntu) pointing at 7f3e2a10-..., the PARTUUID of /dev/nvme0n1p1 in blkid.txt; Boot0001 points at one blkid does not know
- -o sets the new BootOrder: 0003 first, then the old order without it (0000, 0004, 0001, 0002)
