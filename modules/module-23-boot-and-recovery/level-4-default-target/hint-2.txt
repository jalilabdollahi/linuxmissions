  ls -l sysroot/etc/systemd/system/default.target      where it points now
  ls sysroot/usr/lib/systemd/system/*.target            the targets that exist
  ln -sfn TARGET LINKNAME                                make/replace a symlink (-f force, -n do not descend)
The text after "->" is stored as typed and read by the booted machine, from its own
root. So write the path as the server sees it, starting with /usr/lib, even though it
looks broken from here. Check with readlink LINK. More: man ln, man systemd.special
