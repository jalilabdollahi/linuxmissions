`findmnt -F capture/mountinfo` reads a saved mount table (see `man findmnt`;
`-l` gives a plain list, `-n` drops the header, `-o` selects columns such as
TARGET,SOURCE,FSTYPE,VFS-OPTIONS). A bind mount of a subdirectory is shown
as /dev/xxx[/subdir]. When two lines have the same target, the later one
covers the earlier one.
You can also read the raw file: the fields are id, parent, major:minor,
root, mount point, mount options, optional fields, `-`, fstype, source,
super options. Paths with a space are written with \040.
