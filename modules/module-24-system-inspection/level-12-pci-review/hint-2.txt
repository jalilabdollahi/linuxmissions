In `lspci -nnk` the first bracket group `[0200]` is the PCI class (01xx =
storage, 02xx = network), the last one `[8086:158b]` is vendor:device.
`Kernel driver in use:` exists only when a driver is bound;
`Kernel modules:` lists the modules that could handle the device.
awk can read blank-line-separated records with `-v RS=` (paragraph mode)
and `-F'\n'` (each line is a field). Within a record test the header for the
class, test the other lines for the driver, and print the pieces you need;
a loop over the fields works well, and sub() can cut text out of a line.
