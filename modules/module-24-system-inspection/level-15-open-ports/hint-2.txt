`ss -ltnp` = listening, TCP, numeric, with processes (`man ss`; add `-H` to
drop the header). Your own processes show up as `users:(("name",pid=N,fd=M))`;
the port is the text after the last colon of the local address
(`127.0.0.1:8123`, `[::1]:8123`, `*:8123`).
The name in that field and the one in /proc/PID/cmdline are chosen by the
process, but `/proc/PID/exe` is a symlink to the file that is really being
executed: `readlink /proc/PID/exe`.
