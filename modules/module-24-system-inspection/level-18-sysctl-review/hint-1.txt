A grep over all the files shows every place a key is written, but not which
one counts. Two questions decide it: which files are read at all (can one
file hide another?) and in which order are the files applied (the last
assignment wins). Look at the file names across the directories, and be
careful how names sort: is `9-x.conf` before or after `60-y.conf`? Also
watch out for different spellings of the same key.
