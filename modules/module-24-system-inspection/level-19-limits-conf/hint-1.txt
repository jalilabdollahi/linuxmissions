Every process carries its own limits and its own table of open files, and
the kernel shows both under /proc/PID. First find the indexer's PID. Which
file there lists the limits, and which directory holds one entry per open
file? Then think about the fix: who starts the indexer in production? A
setting that the starting program reads at login is not necessarily read
when systemd launches a service. How does systemd let you change a unit
without editing the vendor's file?
