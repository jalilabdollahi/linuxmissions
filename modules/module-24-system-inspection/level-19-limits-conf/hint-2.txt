`/proc/PID/limits` has a line "Max open files" with the soft and the hard
value; `ls /proc/PID/fd | wc -l` counts the open descriptors (`prlimit
--pid PID --nofile` shows the limits too). `/etc/security/limits.conf` is
read by pam_limits when somebody logs in, and a systemd service is not a
login. Services take `LimitNOFILE=` in the `[Service]` section of the unit
or of a drop-in file `unit.service.d/NAME.conf`. Drop-ins are applied in the
order of their names and the last assignment wins: look at what is already
in that directory.
