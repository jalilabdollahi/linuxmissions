`hostname` (or `uname -n`, or `cat /proc/sys/kernel/hostname`) prints the
name the kernel has. To change one line, `sed -i 's/OLD/NEW/' file` works,
but anchor the pattern: `^` matches the start of a line, so `^hostname = `
cannot match a comment, and it cannot match `report_hostname`.
Use `$(hostname)` to put the name into the sed command. Check with
`grep -n hostname agent.conf` afterwards.
