A namespace is identified by an inode number: two processes are in the same
network namespace exactly when their numbers are equal. So first you need to
know which numbers are the HOST's. Which process is certainly in the host's
namespaces? Do not guess by looking for the smallest or biggest number. Then
compare every process against the host's PID and network numbers: which
combination are you looking for, and which two opposite combinations look
similar but do not count?
