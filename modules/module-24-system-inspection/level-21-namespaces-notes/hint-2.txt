In lsns.json each namespace appears once, with the lowest PID in it: pick
the `pid` and `net` entries whose `pid` is 1 (`jq -r '.namespaces[] |
select(.type=="net" and .pid==1) | .ns'`). ps.txt has one row per process
with the two inode numbers in columns 3 (PIDNS) and 4 (NETNS), after a
header line. awk can pass the host values in with `-v` and compare the
columns: PIDNS different from the host's, NETNS equal to the host's. A
process with the host's PID namespace but its own network (a debug
container started with --pid=host) is the opposite case. `sort -n` orders
by PID.
