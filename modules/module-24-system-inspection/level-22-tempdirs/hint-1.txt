`du` only counts files that still have a name. Space can also be held by a
file that was deleted while a process still has it open: the data stays until
the last descriptor is closed. Which process would hold something like
that, and where does the kernel list the files a process has open? The order
of your steps matters here: once the holder exits, the data is gone for good.
For the cleanup, think about which old files must not be deleted even if
they are old.
