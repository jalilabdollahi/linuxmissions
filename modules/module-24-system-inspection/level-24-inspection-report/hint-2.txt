`uname -n`, `uname -r`, `uname -m`; `. /etc/os-release` loads `ID` and
`VERSION_ID` into your shell (it handles the quotes); `nproc`; `awk` on
`/proc/meminfo` (MemTotal is in kB) and on `/proc/uptime` (first field);
`df -P /` has the percentage in the fifth column of its second line.
`ss -ltnH` lists listeners without a header: take the text after the
last colon of the local address, then `sort -un`.
`jq -n --arg name "text"` passes a string, `--argjson name 42` passes a
JSON value such as a number or an array. For the ports build the array
with `jq -Rnc '[inputs | tonumber]'`, which gives [] for empty input
(the naive `[$x]` trick gives [""] ).
