`who FILE` reads another utmp file (see `man who`). The source host is
printed in parentheses at the end of the line. Local console logins have
no parentheses at all.
Use awk to take the last field and test it; remember that matching the text
"10" is not the same as "starts with 10 followed by a dot": 110.x.x.x and
210.x.x.x contain "10" too. Anchor the pattern at the start of the address.
