`w` prints idle time as `45.00s` (seconds), `3:12` (minutes:seconds),
`1:05m` (hours:minutes, note the m) and `2days`. Only the first two
shapes can be under ten minutes, and for `min:sec` only the part before
the colon matters.
awk can split the IDLE field (column 5) with `split(field, parts, ":")`.
Skip the first two lines (the header). Finish with `sort -u` so each user
appears once.
