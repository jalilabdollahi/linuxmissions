`pgrep -f PATTERN` matches against the whole command line; `pgrep -af`
shows the command lines so you can pick the right process (worker.py, not
worker-metrics.py). The environment of PID n is the file /proc/n/environ.
It is one long line: the entries are separated by NUL bytes, not newlines,
so convert them first with `tr '\0' '\n'`. Then keep only the two variables,
and anchor the match: DB_HOST_FALLBACK and DB_HOSTNAME are different
variables.
