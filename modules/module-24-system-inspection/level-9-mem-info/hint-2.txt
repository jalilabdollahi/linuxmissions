MemAvailable is the kernel's own estimate of memory available to
applications. All sizes in meminfo are in kB: divide by 1024 for MiB (awk
`int($2 / 1024)` rounds down).
Reserved huge pages are HugePages_Total multiplied by Hugepagesize, and
Hugepagesize is not always 2048 kB. Compare that with Shmem and
SUnreclaim. awk can remember the values as it reads each file and decide in
an END block.
