  base64 -d             decode standard base64 (needs the normal alphabet and = padding)
  tr '_-' '/+'          turn the URL-safe alphabet back into the standard one
  ${#var}               length of a shell variable; base64 text length must be a multiple of 4, fill with =
  gunzip                decompress gzip data from stdin (`file -` tells you what a result is)
Three layers: look at the result of each decode with `file` or `head -c 80`. More: man base64
