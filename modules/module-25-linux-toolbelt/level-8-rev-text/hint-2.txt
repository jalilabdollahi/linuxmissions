  sed -E 's#...#...#'      strip the scheme, then everything from the first / or ?, then a :port, then a trailing dot
  tr A-Z a-z               hostnames are case-insensitive
  rev                      reverses each line, so the last labels come first
  cut -d. -f1-2            keeps the first two dot-separated fields (use -f1-3 for the co.uk family)
  sort | uniq -c           counts equal lines; sort -k1,1nr orders by that count
