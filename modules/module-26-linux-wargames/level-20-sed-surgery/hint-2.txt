`sed` accepts an address range before a command: `/start/,/end/{ ... }` applies what is inside the braces only between those lines. Anchor the headers with `^\[tls\]` so `[tls_client]` does not match. Use `-E` for extended regex and `[[:space:]]*` around the `=`, since some files have no spaces; capture the key part in a group and reuse it with `\1`. `-i.bak` edits in place and keeps the original. For CRLF files avoid `.*` at the end of the match: it swallows the carriage return (`[^[:cntrl:]]*` stops before it). Use `cat -A` to see line endings and `diff conf/x.conf.bak conf/x.conf` to review a change.
