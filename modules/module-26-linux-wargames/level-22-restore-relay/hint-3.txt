Check every archive, pick the good Tuesday copy by its time, and extract the chain in order:
  for a in backups/*.tar offsite/*.bak; do tar -tf "$a" >/dev/null 2>&1 && echo "ok $a" || echo "DAMAGED $a"; done
  good=$(ls -l --time-style=long-iso offsite/*.bak | grep '2026-11-10 18:02' | awk '{print $NF}')
  mkdir -p restored
  for a in backups/share-full-2026-11-08.tar backups/share-inc-2026-11-09.tar "$good"; do tar -x --listed-incremental=/dev/null -f "$a" -C restored; done
The loop shows which archive is damaged (Tuesday's in backups/); the second line finds the offsite copy with Tuesday 18:02's time; the last loop extracts Sunday, Monday and Tuesday in order, and --listed-incremental=/dev/null lets tar apply each archive's deletions. Wednesday's archive is left out on purpose.
