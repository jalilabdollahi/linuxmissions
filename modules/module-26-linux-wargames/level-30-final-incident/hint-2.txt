`crontab -l` and `ls etc/cron.d` show the schedules; the cron log in var/log/cron.log shows what ran and when. A crontab can be rewritten from a pipe: `crontab -l | grep -v PATTERN | crontab -`. `find ... -mtime +N -delete` applies an age policy (RETENTION.md), and `-maxdepth 1 -name` keeps you out of subdirectories like audit/. `ssh-keygen -lf FILE.pub` prints a key's fingerprint; compare it with the audit logs to find the deploy and match the key body (field 2 of a line) in authorized_keys files and configs, ignoring the comment. `grep -vF` removes lines literally. Check your work by watching `du` or the cron log for a few seconds.
