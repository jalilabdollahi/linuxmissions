Stop the cron job in both places, clean per retention, revoke the key, restart the shop and record the facts:
  crontab -l | grep -v cache-warm | crontab -
  rm etc/cron.d/warmup
  find var/cache/shop -maxdepth 1 -name 'warm-*.bin' -delete
  find var/spool/shop -name 'orders-*.jsonl' -mtime +3 -delete
  fp=$(ssh-keygen -lf etc/shop/deploy_key.pub | awk '{print $2}'); body=$(awk '{print $2}' etc/shop/deploy_key.pub)
  id=$(grep -l "$fp" var/cache/shop/audit/*.log | sed 's|.*/deploy-||; s|\.log$||')
  first=$(date -d "$(grep cache-warm var/log/cron.log | head -1 | awk '{print $1, $2, "2026", $3}')" '+%F %H:%M')
  for f in home/deploy/.ssh/authorized_keys home/ci/.ssh/authorized_keys etc/shop/deploy.conf; do grep -vF "$body" "$f" > "$f.new" && mv "$f.new" "$f"; done
  rm etc/shop/deploy_key etc/shop/deploy_key.pub
  systemctl restart shop
  jq -n --arg t "$first" --arg fp "$fp" --arg id "$id" '{first_bad_cron_run:$t, key_fingerprint:$fp, deploy_id:$id, cause_chain:["leaked-key","bad-deploy","cron-every-minute","disk-full","app-down"]}' > postmortem.json
The first two lines remove the runaway job from the user crontab and from etc/cron.d (the legitimate jobs stay); the finds delete the cache chunks and the orders older than three days but leave audit/ alone; the next lines take the leaked key's fingerprint and body, find the deploy whose audit log used it, and the first run in the cron log; the loop drops the key from the three places that list it and rm removes the key files; systemctl restart brings the shop back; jq writes the postmortem.
