setfacl --restore=perms.acl
find app -newer perms.acl -type d -exec chmod 2750 {} +
find app -newer perms.acl -type f -exec chmod 640 {} +
find app -newer perms.acl -type f -path '*/bin/*' -exec chmod 750 {} +
find app -newer perms.acl -type f -path '*/secrets/*' -exec chmod 600 {} +

- setfacl --restore=perms.acl   puts every dumped path back: mode, special bits and named-user entries, spaces in names included
- find -newer perms.acl -type d   only paths created after the dump (the old ones keep their old mtime); directories get 2750
- the next three lines   files get 640, then bin/ files 750 and secrets/ files 600, most specific last
