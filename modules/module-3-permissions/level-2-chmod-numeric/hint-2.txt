In octal mode each digit is one group (owner, group, others), and the digit is
the sum of: read = 4, write = 2, execute = 1.
  rw- = 4+2   = 6
  r-x = 4+1   = 5
  r-- = 4     = 4
  --- = 0
chmod takes the three digits: chmod 640 file  means rw- r-- ---
Check your work with ls -l, or stat -c '%a %n' * (on macOS: stat -f '%Lp %N' *).
More: man chmod
