Finding it:
  ps aux | grep debug_logger      full process lines (2nd column = PID)
  pgrep -f debug_logger.sh        only the PIDs; -f matches the whole command line
Stopping it:
  kill PID                        sends SIGTERM, a polite "please exit"
  kill -9 PID                     SIGKILL, only if SIGTERM is ignored
  pkill -f PATTERN                find and signal in one step; be specific!
More: man kill, man pgrep
