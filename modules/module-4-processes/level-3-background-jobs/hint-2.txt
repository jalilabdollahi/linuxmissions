Job control in bash:
  CMD &         start CMD in the background, prompt returns at once
  jobs          list this shell's jobs: [1]+ Running  ./process.sh &
  jobs -l       the same with each job's PID
  wait          block until all background jobs have finished
  fg %1         bring job 1 to the foreground (also waits for it)
You can put several commands on one line separated by ; or newlines.
More: help jobs, help wait   (they are shell builtins, so help, not man)
