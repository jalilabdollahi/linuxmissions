Edit the file:  edit listener.conf   (or sed -i.bak 's/old/new/' file)
Send a signal:  kill -SIGNAL PID
  kill -HUP PID          SIGHUP: "reload your config" for most daemons
  kill -l                list all signal names and numbers
  cat listener.pid       the listener's PID (pgrep -f listener.sh works too)
Plain kill (SIGTERM) or kill -9 would stop the service instead.
More: man kill, man 7 signal
