ps can print exactly the columns you choose:
  ps -eo pid,ppid,stat,comm      every process: PID, parent PID, state, name
STAT starting with Z = zombie. Keep only those lines with awk:
  ... | awk '$3 ~ /^Z/'          (column 3 starts with Z)
Then look at the parent before you kill it:
  ps -o pid,args -p PPID         is it job_runner.py or something else?
  kill PPID                      SIGTERM to the parent
More: man ps (look for "PROCESS STATE CODES" and "-o")
