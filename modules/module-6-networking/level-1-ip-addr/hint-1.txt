Open ip_addr.txt and look at how the blocks are built: a numbered header line per interface, then indented lines for its link and addresses.

Which lines carry IPv4 addresses, and how do they differ from the IPv6 ones? Look at the END of those lines too: does an address line already tell you which interface it belongs to? Check eno1 (two addresses), eno1.120@eno1 and eno2 (none).
