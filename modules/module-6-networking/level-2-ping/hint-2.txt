- `ping -c 1 HOST` sends one packet and stops. `-W 1` waits at most one second for the reply.
- `if COMMAND; then ... fi` runs the branch when COMMAND exits with 0. You don't need to read ping's text: the exit status says it all. Add `>/dev/null 2>&1` to hide the output.
- `while read -r host; do ... done < hosts.txt` loops over the lines of the file.
- Skip comment and blank lines: `case "$host" in ''|'#'*) continue ;; esac`
- Redirect the whole loop once: `done < hosts.txt > reachable.txt`

Read more: `man ping` (-c, -W, EXIT STATUS), `help while`.
