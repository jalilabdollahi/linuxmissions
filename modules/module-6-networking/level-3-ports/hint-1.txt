Only one State in ss-output.txt means "waiting for connections". The other rows are connections that already exist (and their local port can be anything).

For each listening row, look at the Local Address:Port column. The address part says WHO can connect: 127.0.0.1, 127.0.0.53 and [::1] only accept connections from the machine itself. Also notice that IPv6 addresses contain colons too, so "the port" is whatever comes after the LAST colon.
