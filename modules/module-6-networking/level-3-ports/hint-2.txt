- awk fields: $1 is the State, $4 is Local Address:Port.
- Filter rows: `$1 == "LISTEN" && $4 !~ /^(127\.|\[::1\])/` keeps listening sockets that are not loopback-only.
- `n = split($4, a, ":")` splits at every colon and returns how many parts there are; a[n] is the last part, the port. That also works for "[::]:22".
- `sort -nu` sorts numerically and removes duplicates (22 is listed for IPv4 and IPv6).

Read more: `man awk` (split, ~ and !~), `man sort` (-n, -u). On a live system: `ss -tln` (TCP, listening, numeric).
