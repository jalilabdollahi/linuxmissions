Let journalctl do the filtering with its own options:
  -u UNIT         only one service, e.g. -u myapp.service (or just -u myapp)
  -p LEVEL        that priority and everything more serious: emerg(0) alert(1) crit(2)
                  err(3) warning(4) notice(5) info(6) debug(7)
  --since TIME    "14:00", "2024-04-08 14:00", "30 min ago", "today"
Combine all three, then redirect the output into the file.
Read more: man journalctl (OPTIONS: -p, -S), man systemd.time for the time formats
