./journalctl -u myapp.service -p err --since "14:00" > errors.txt

- -u myapp.service   only entries from that unit (nginx and sshd errors are left out)
- -p err             priority 3 and more serious: catches the crit "Out of memory" line,
                     skips info lines like "error_rate=0.02"
- --since "14:00"    today from 14:00 on (yesterday's 14:10 entry is left out)
- > errors.txt       save the result; check it with: cat errors.txt
