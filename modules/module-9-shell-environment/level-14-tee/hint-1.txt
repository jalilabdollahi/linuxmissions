Run ./gen-events twice and compare a few lines: the run= field and the events
change every time. So three separate `./gen-events | ...` commands will never
agree with each other.

You need one stream that is written to a file AND passed on to the next
command, like a T-junction in a water pipe. Then the same trick a second time
for the filtered lines.

Also look closely at the info events: some of them mention an error in their
msg text. Only the level field counts.
