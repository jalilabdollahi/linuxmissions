- `tee FILE` copies its input to FILE and also to its own stdout, so it can sit
  in the middle of a pipeline:   producer | tee copy.txt | consumer
- You can use tee more than once in the same pipeline.
- `grep 'level=error'` keeps only the lines whose level field is error
  (a plain `grep error` also catches "retrying after upstream error").
- `wc -l` counts the lines it reads; `> file` saves the number.
- tee overwrites its file; `tee -a` would append to it instead.

Read more: man tee
