./gen-events | tee events.raw | grep 'level=error' | tee errors.log | wc -l > error_count.txt

- ./gen-events               one capture of the bus
- | tee events.raw           save every line, and pass every line on
- | grep 'level=error'       keep the error events only
- | tee errors.log           save those, and pass them on
- | wc -l > error_count.txt  count them and save the number

Check: head -3 errors.log; cat error_count.txt
