- 'single quotes': everything inside is literal. No spaces split, no $ expansion. Only a ' itself cannot appear inside.
- "double quotes": spaces and ( ) are safe, but $name is still expanded. "price$list.csv" becomes price.csv.
- \ escapes the one character after it: it\'s\ done.txt
- For the apostrophe, use double quotes ("it's done.txt") or an escape.
- Tab completion writes the escapes for you: type `mv uploads/Q3` and press Tab.
- Preview first: `ls -l 'uploads/Q3 report (final).pdf'`

Read more: `man bash`, section QUOTING.
