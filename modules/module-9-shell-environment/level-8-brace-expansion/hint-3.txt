Type these at the prompt:

mkdir -p data/2026/{01..12} svc/{api,web,worker}/{dev,prod}/{conf,logs}
cp config.yaml{,.bak}

- data/2026/{01..12}                         12 words: data/2026/01 ... data/2026/12, zero-padded
- svc/{api,web,worker}/{dev,prod}/{conf,logs} 3 x 2 x 2 = 12 paths, every combination
- mkdir -p                                   also creates data/, svc/ and the middle levels
- cp config.yaml{,.bak}                      expands to: cp config.yaml config.yaml.bak

Check: find data svc -type d | sort
